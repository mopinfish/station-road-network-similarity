# Separate station correspondence

Station group codes derive from 駅データ.jp, free edition 2026-07-31: https://www.ekidata.jp/ . Original terms: https://www.ekidata.jp/agreement.php . Those terms permit use, processing and third-party supply; unprocessed supply must be free of charge and the listed prohibited uses remain applicable. This table is proposed for free supply. No replacement MIT or ODbL grant is made for provider contents.

road_id joins independently numbered road records; g_cd joins the existing station/quality tables. offset_m preserves the adopted station-representative-to-road-origin offset. Existing representative coordinates remain in the separate station supplement. This table is not inside the ODbL road archive.
